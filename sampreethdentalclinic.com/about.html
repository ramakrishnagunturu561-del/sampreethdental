Our Story & Standards

Team-Based, Comprehensive Dental Healthcare

At Sampreeth Dental Clinic, we provide team-based, patient-centered dental care for both children and adults. We specialize in managing complex clinical cases, ranging from anxious patients requiring gentle pain-free anesthesia to intricate maxillofacial bone reconstructions and full-mouth rehabilitations.

Oral health is an indispensable pillar of systemic health and self-confidence. Our clinic brings together state-of-the-art diagnostic facilities, modern digital radiography, multi-cycle autoclave sterilization, and dedicated surgical operatory rooms.

Maxillofacial Specialist

Dr. Nadella Koteswara Rao

MDS – Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

Consultant Surgeon & Implantologist

Dr. Nadella Koteswara Rao is a distinguished Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon known for his surgical precision and clinical acumen. He has extensive expertise in treating complex facial bone fractures, surgical removal of impacted third molars (wisdom teeth), management of jaw pathology (dentigerous cysts and benign tumors), and osseointegrated dental implantology.

Core Areas of Practice:

  • Impacted Teeth Excision
  • Dental Implant Fixtures
  • Mandibular & Maxillary Fractures
  • Jaw Cysts & Neoplasms
  • Bone Grafting & Sinus Lift
  • Temporomandibular Joint (TMJ)
Available for Surgical Consultation
Restorative & Aesthetic Specialist

Dr. Nadella Neelima

BDS – Dental Surgeon

Aesthetic Dentist & Endodontist

Dr. Nadella Neelima brings a warm, patient-first approach to general, pediatric, and cosmetic dentistry. Core Areas of Practice:

  • Laser Teeth Whitening
  • Pediatric & Teen Dental Care

  • Flap Surgery & Gum Disease
  • Removable & Fixed Dentures
