Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon

Dr. Nadella Koteswara Rao

MDS – Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

Consultant Surgeon & Implantologist

Dr. Nadella Koteswara Rao is a distinguished Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon known for surgical precision and clinical acumen. He has extensive expertise in treating complex facial bone trauma, surgical removal of impacted third molars (wisdom teeth), management of jaw pathology (dentigerous cysts and benign tumors), and osseointegrated dental implantology.

Core Areas of Practice:

  • Impacted Teeth Excision
  • Dental Implant Fixtures
  • Mandibular & Maxillary Fractures
  • Jaw Cysts & Neoplasms
  • Bone Grafting & Sinus Lift
  • Temporomandibular Joint (TMJ)
