Aesthetic Care

1. Teeth Whitening & Ultrasonic Scaling

Teeth often accumulate extrinsic stains and discoloration over time due to tea, coffee, smoking, red wine, medications, or natural aging. At Sampreeth Dental Clinic, we utilize scientifically proven bleaching agents (hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide) that gently break down stubborn stain compounds into micro-particles without eroding or thinning your enamel.

Removes deep calculus & tartar buildup
Up to 3 to 5 shades brighter in one sitting
Enamel-safe with zero sensitivity protocols
Ultrasonic painless scaling technology

Permanent Tooth Replacement

2. Dental Implants (Titanium & Zirconia)

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth lost to decay, injury, or periodontal infection. An implant is a biocompatible titanium fixture placed securely into the jawbone that undergoes biological osseointegration, fusing naturally with bone to form a permanent root substitute. Once healed, a custom zirconia crown is mounted for a result virtually indistinguishable from a natural tooth.

Prevents facial bone loss & sunken cheeks
Restores 100% natural chewing strength
No grinding of adjacent healthy teeth needed
Lifetime durability with proper hygiene

Consult an Implant Specialist

Performed by Dr. Nadella Koteswara Rao (MDS Oral Surgeon).

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

3. Surgical Removal of Impacted Teeth

When third molars (wisdom teeth) lack sufficient room to erupt upright, they become impacted—angled horizontally, pressing against adjacent roots, or trapped within bone. This frequently leads to severe recurrent infections (pericoronitis), gum swelling, cysts, and crowding of front teeth. Dr. Nadella Koteswara Rao specializes in performing gentle, atraumatic wisdom tooth extractions with minimal post-operative downtime.

Digital X-Ray / OPG nerve tracing before surgery
Pain-free local anesthesia & sedation support
Safe preservation of the inferior alveolar nerve
Rapid healing with PRF / membrane placement

Wisdom Tooth Pain?

Same-day relief and evaluation by our Maxillofacial Surgeon.

Pathology Management

4. Removal of Jaw Cysts & Benign Tumors

Odontogenic cysts (such as dentigerous cysts, radicular cysts, or keratocysts) develop silently around unerupted crowns or infected roots, expanding within the jawbone and weakening structural integrity. We perform thorough enucleation, marsupialization, and complete surgical excision coupled with bone grafting to restore full structural bone density.

Biopsy and histopathological verification
Complete cyst cavity eradication
Structural bone graft reconstruction
Regular radiologic follow-up monitoring

Swelling or Jaw Lump?

Early clinical diagnosis prevents extensive bone loss.

Facial Trauma & Emergency

5. Fractured Jaw Bone Fixation (Maxillofacial Trauma)

Mandibular and maxillary fractures caused by road accidents, sports injuries, or falls require specialized surgical realignment to restore normal dental occlusion (bite alignment) and facial symmetry. Dr. Koteswara Rao employs titanium miniplate osteosynthesis (rigid internal fixation) ensuring early jaw mobilization and swift functional recovery.

Anatomical reduction of mandibular condyle & body
Titanium miniplate & screw rigid fixation
Restores full bite alignment & speech clarity
Emergency maxillofacial trauma care available

Endodontic Care

6. Painless Root Canal Treatments (RCT)

When deep dental caries (tooth decay) reaches the pulp chamber, severe pain and abscess formation occur. Our modern rotary endodontics protocol completely clears infected nerve tissue, disinfects the microscopic canals with biocompatible irrigants, seals them with gutta-percha, and seals the tooth with a lifelike crown—saving your natural tooth for decades.

Single-sitting RCT options available
Digital apex locators for precision canal measuring
Painless anesthesia delivery system
CAD/CAM Zirconia or PFM crown coverage

Toothache Relief

Instant relief from throbbing dental pain and sensitivity.

Periodontics

9. Periodontal Cleaning & Flap Surgeries

Periodontitis (gum disease) causes bleeding gums, bad breath (halitosis), bone recession, and loose teeth if left untreated. Our periodontic interventions include subgingival curettage, deep root planing, pocket reduction flap surgeries, and bone grafting to arrest disease progression and tighten loose teeth.

Stops bleeding gums and chronic bad breath
Regenerative bone grafting for lost alveolar bone
Pocket depth reduction procedures
Laser-assisted gum sterilization

Bleeding or Swollen Gums?
